[ 04 ] THE COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE

it's the trader and their experience.

A strategy is just a hypothesis. The real alpha comes from thousands of regime-tagged trades revealing what actually works â and what doesn't â in each specific market condition. Every trade the fleet takes gets stamped with the full Aquila state: macro regime, GEX regime, intraday transition phase. Over time, assumptions get replaced by empirical truth.

When Echo discovers that mean-reversion win rate drops below 50% during Rate Shock, the whole fleet knows. When Surge finds that momentum trades during the first hour of No Shelter have 3x the R:R of afternoon entries â that insight propagates. The fleet gets smarter every single day.
