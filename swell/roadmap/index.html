Strategy Selection Matrix
Low IV + High ConvictionNaked LEAPS. Cheapest way to ride an early theme before anyone's buying options on it.
High IV + High ConvictionBull call spreads. Cap IV risk while maintaining directional exposure.
Medium SignalsWatchlist only. No trade. Wait for confirmation.
ENTRY PHILOSOPHY Don't hedge early signals. Swell's edge is catching themes before they're priced in. IV is usually still low when the signal fires. Naked LEAPS or bull call spreads are the sweet spot.
EXIT PHILOSOPHY Hedging makes more sense for exits. When Swell shows a theme peaking and you want to protect gains, transition into hedged structures. Detecting peak social momentum is the harder and more valuable problem.
